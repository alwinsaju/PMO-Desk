PMO work arrives as flags, pings and "can you just..."

Requests land in flagged emails and Teams messages, scattered across the day. A flag you meant to come back to scrolls out of sight. Then Friday arrives, and the status report has to be rebuilt from memory.

What it does

From a flag to a task, with you in the loop

You keep working where you already work. picks up only what you flag or send, and proposes a task for you to check.

STEP 1

You flag or send

Flag an email in Outlook, or use "Send to" on a Teams message.

STEP 2

A local AI reads it

A model on your laptop drafts a title, owner, due date and priority, with the quote it used.

STEP 3

It lands in a queue

The proposal waits in your Review Queue. It is not a task yet.

STEP 4

You approve

Approve, edit or reject. This is the only gate.

STEP 5

It joins your list

The task shows up on your To-Do list, board and calendar, with reminders.

Three promises

Built so you can trust it with work messages

The AI runs on your laptop

Messages are read by a model running locally through Ollama, on your own computer. Internal message text is not sent to any cloud AI.

Nothing happens without your approval

The review queue is the gate. The AI only proposes. A message never becomes a task until you press Approve.

It only reads what you choose

It cannot browse your mailbox. It sees a message only when you flag it or send it. Teams items are blocked unless your Teams tenant or that chat is on your allowlist.

Honest fine print

What to expect before you try it

It needs a decent laptop

Tuned on a laptop with 31 GB of RAM and no graphics card. A local model is memory hungry.

It is an inbox, not a chat

The local model takes roughly a minute per message, and about 2 minutes for the first, cold one. It works in the background while you do other things.

Windows only, for now

There is no Mac or phone version. The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may show a warning on first run.

One-off setup

Flagging and Teams "Send to" need a small Power Automate flow in your own Microsoft 365 account. A guide takes about 45 minutes the first time, and we will help.

Pilot software

Expect rough edges. The model will sometimes be wrong, which is why you approve everything.

Not an official product

This is an unofficial internal pilot. Confirm data handling with firm IT and risk before wider use.

FAQ

Questions people ask first

Where does my data go?

The message text is read by an AI model on your own laptop. Your tasks, queue and settings are stored on your computer, and the app's own servers listen only on your machine. The small Power Automate flow that hands over a flagged message runs inside your own Microsoft 365 account, like any flow you build there.

Will it send emails for me?

PMO To-Do never sends mail. Drafts such as chasers open unsent in your mail app for you to read and send. Separately, the Tasks automations for recurring reports can send through your Outlook, but only after you reach the confirm step and press it.

Can it see my whole mailbox?

No. It reads only items you flag or send, and has no access to the rest of your inbox. For Teams, an item is ignored unless your tenant or that specific chat is on your allowlist.

Who sees my data?

Only you, on your laptop. There is no shared server for the pilot. Feedback you give us in the chat is whatever you choose to tell us. The optional diagnostics file for support has passwords, tokens, emails and user names removed.

What if the AI is wrong?

That is what the review queue is for. Every proposal shows the quote it came from, so you can check it quickly. Edit it or reject it. We measure how often you accept proposals unedited, so we learn where it falls short.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows laptop with plenty of memory (we tuned on 31 GB, no graphics card), Outlook desktop, Microsoft Teams, and permission to install software. Setup installs the local AI runtime and model for you, and the first run takes a while.
